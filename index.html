The Little Things That Became Everything

♥

Hey Bhavana, it has been more than a year since you came into my life, and I feel incredibly lucky to have you. This has been the most beautiful phase of my life, and sometimes I still can't believe you're mine.

I still remember seeing you for the first time on June 21, the day of my interview. From that moment, you became my crush — and even after we started our relationship, I never stopped crushing on you.

After I joined the organization, I kept admiring you and secretly waiting for the moment you'd look at me.

Somehow, it feels like the universe planned everything for us. Everything happened like a little miracle, and our journey began. ❤️

Since that day, every little moment with you has become special — our first chat, that beautiful night when we expressed our feelings, our first hand-hold, midnight walk, hug, kiss, and all the little things that followed.

I love the way you hold my hand, lean on my shoulder, look at me with love, and sometimes give me that wonderfully creepy look. 😂❤️

I love the child in you too — the one who gets excited over little things, laughs without even trying, and makes even the simplest moments feel special. I love that wild, carefree side of you, the part of you that is just completely yourself. And honestly, I hope you never lose that. ❤️

You notice everything about me; you comfort me when I'm sad, celebrate harder when I'm happy, and take care of me when I'm struggling.

You changed me from Adivi Manishi to City Manishi. 😂

Our silly fights somehow make us stronger, and your belief in me makes me believe in myself.

I'm sorry for all the times I've hurt you — although I know after reading this, you may want to hit me anyway. 😂

That's your Wild Love... your Doola Daanaaaaa. ❤️

I never imagined someone could love me this much. And honestly, sometimes I wonder how someone like you can love someone like me this deeply, even when I know I don't always deserve it.

I don't even do some of the most basic things I should. I forget to ask if you ate, why you haven't eaten, whether you're feeling okay, or if you took your medicine when you're sick. Sometimes I even forget that you are going through something, while you quietly keep tolerating and adjusting with me. And that truth makes me realise how much more I should be there for you.

I know there are so many people who could choose you, love you, and give you everything you deserve. Still, you chose me. Even when I struggle to keep the balance in our bond, you keep choosing us. That means more to me than I know how to say.

Most of the time, I imagine so many things in my head. I feel them deeply, but I'm not always good at expressing them. There is so much inside me that stays unsaid because I simply don't know how to put it into words.

Because of my behaviour, I know you have had to adjust, understand me, and sometimes carry more than you should. I don't always know how to fix all the chaos I create, but somehow you still find a way to make things okay. I am genuinely lucky to have you.

And maybe that is one of the things I will always be grateful for — even after seeing all my flaws, you still choose me, still stay with me, and still believe in us. ❤️

When I went home, I missed you more than I expected. I thought I would stay away for a while, but within just ten days, I came back to Hyderabad — on Yamini’s birthday. And the way you treated me that day told me everything. I could see how much you had missed me too. I felt so happy, so loved, and honestly, I just enjoyed being with you again. ❤️

There is one funny thing I always remember. I once saw a reel where a mother was playing with her little baby, saying things like, “I’ll eat you,” “I’ll bite you,” and making those silly little sounds because she loved her baby so much. Then she realised that sometimes, when you love someone more than you can explain, it comes out in the strangest and cutest ways.

It always makes me think of you. I remember the first time I asked you to bite me and you didn’t. 😂 But now you come close and try to bite me without me even asking. And every time you do that, I can't help but think about how much you love me and how completely obsessed you are with me. 😂❤️ Maybe that is your own little way of saying, “You are mine, and I love you so much.”

There is one more thing I have to admit — I am especially a fan of your smile. Actuallyyyyy... a big fan. ❤️ I love your smile, your eyes, and even that small mole on the left side of your neck. Whenever I see you, somehow I notice all these little things without even trying, and I fall for you all over again. If you call this obsession or kamam, I will accept it. 😂 Because yes, you really do stay in my mind. Whenever I close my eyes and imagine you, I see your face with that beautiful smile. And when you smile wholeheartedly, your eyes glow a little more — actually, a lot more. I don't know how to explain it, but I just stay there in that moment, completely lost in you. ❤️ Your smile is always beautiful, but I love it the most when you do something doola and then smile without holding anything back — that open, happy smile full of love. That is the smile I could keep looking at forever. ❤️ Every gift and every little thing from you means more to me than I can explain.

But more than all the gifts, I love the person who gave them to me.

Love youuuuu, my love — Naa Pilla. ❤️
