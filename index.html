The Little Things That Became Everything

♥

Hey Bhavana, it has been more than a year since you came into my life, and I feel incredibly lucky to have you. This has been the most beautiful phase of my life, and sometimes I still can't believe you're mine.

I still remember seeing you for the first time on June 21, the day of my interview. From that moment, you became my crush — and even after we started our relationship, I never stopped crushing on you.

After I joined the organization, I kept admiring you and secretly waiting for the moment you'd look at me.

Somehow, it feels like the universe planned everything for us. Everything happened like a little miracle, and our journey began. ❤️

Since that day, every little moment with you has become special — our first chat, that beautiful night when we expressed our feelings, our first hand-hold, midnight walk, hug, kiss, and all the little things that followed.

I love the way you hold my hand, lean on my shoulder, look at me with love, and sometimes give me that wonderfully creepy look. 😂❤️

You notice everything about me; you comfort me when I'm sad, celebrate harder when I'm happy, and take care of me when I'm struggling.

You changed me from Adivi Manishi to City Manishi. 😂

Our silly fights somehow make us stronger, and your belief in me makes me believe in myself.

I'm sorry for all the times I've hurt you — although I know after reading this, you may want to hit me anyway. 😂

That's your Wild Love... your Doola Daanaaaaa. ❤️

I never imagined someone could love me this much.

Every gift and every little thing from you means more to me than I can explain.

But more than all the gifts, I love the person who gave them to me.

Love youuuuu, my love — Naa Pilla. ❤️
